# STASH Video Design

## Style Prompt
Keep STASH's own neo-brutalist product identity: warm-white grid canvas, heavy near-black borders and hard shadows, oversized uppercase Space Grotesk headings, lime interaction surfaces, with restrained pink and lavender savings accents. Treat the product like a serious fintech launch while keeping its playful copy visible. Use the phone sandbox as the hero visual and preserve legibility at 1920x1080.

## Colors
- Warm white background: #f6f6f6
- Near-black text and borders: #0c0f0f
- Lime primary accent: #cafd00
- Lavender secondary accent: #bba2ff
- Pink savings accent: #ffbdf3

## Typography
- Space Grotesk for display and UI headings
- Plus Jakarta Sans for supporting body copy

## What NOT to Do
- Do not use generic blue SaaS styling.
- Do not replace the phone sandbox with abstract graphics.
- Do not add unsupported financial claims or fake metrics.
- Do not overload the parody with sound effects.
