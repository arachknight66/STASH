# Brag Plan: STASH

## What is this app?
STASH is a privacy-first personal finance tracker that lets people log purchases, track savings goals, and audit subscriptions without linking a bank account.

## The angle
A dead-serious fintech launch for the least serious purchase: one $6.50 boba. Show the actual clickable sandbox logic as a tiny money-management victory, in STASH's loud neo-brutalist colors and copy.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The exact hero line slams in: “GET STASHED. NOT STRAPPED.” followed by a small “INTERACTIVE PRODUCT SANDBOX” tag. Lime, black borders, and hard shadows make it unmistakably STASH.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Recreate the sandbox phone with its real $12,450.00 Liquidity Vault, Bali Getaway savings card, and Recent Ledger.
- Simulate clicking “Log $6.50 Boba”; the balance ticks to $12,443.50 and “Matcha Latte” appears in the ledger, matching the app's actual behavior.
- Hold the real claim “Strict Privacy (Zero Bank Syncs)” beside the product UI.

## Outro / punchline
“NO BANK LOGINS. NO SPREADSHEETS.” then STASH wordmark. The first phrase is grounded in the app; “No spreadsheets” is from the README.

## User flow worth showing
Open the interactive product sandbox → click “Log $6.50 Boba” → see the reduced balance and new Matcha Latte ledger entry.

## Tone
- Preset: yc-parody
- Creative direction: tiny impulse purchase, treated like an institutional finance breakthrough
- Interpretation: keep the edit composed and serious, with restrained impact sounds and crisp hard cuts; let the product's Gen Z language carry the humor.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #f6f6f6
- Accent: #cafd00 lime; supporting lavender #bba2ff and pink #ffbdf3
- Text: #0c0f0f
- Display font: Space Grotesk
- Body font: Plus Jakarta Sans
- Strongest visual element: high-contrast black-bordered phone mockup, lime button, pink Bali goal card, and rigid neo-brutalist shadows.

## Share copy (draft)
We built STASH to track your money without linking your bank. One $6.50 boba, logged. The spreadsheets can stay closed.

## Audio direction
- Role: restrained parody bed with a few dry UI accents
- Music: Happy Beats / Business Moves vol. 9 by ende.app
- Music treatment: start at 0s, volume 0.18, gentle fade-in over 0.3s and fade under the last word; no dramatic swell.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset at `/home/arachknight/.codex/skills/brag/assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`; tempo 114.84 BPM. Strong cues near 3.70s (hook landing), 10.54s (click/result), and 19.48s (outro). Use cues as optional timing hints only.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let the lime vault card presence breathe slightly with the bed, no waveform graphics.
- SFX posture: sparse; one restrained click and one soft reveal hit.
- Audio-coupled moments: simulated click on “Log $6.50 Boba”; balance/ledger result lands near a strong cue.
- Restraint rule: no sound gag or SFX pile-on; the joke is the scale of the ceremony around a boba.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Institutional-grade boba — 3s
Lime tag “INTERACTIVE PRODUCT SANDBOX” appears, then “GET STASHED. NOT STRAPPED.” lands huge over the source grid texture. Treat it like a major fintech announcement.
Sequential/interaction: title and tag reveal in order; hold the full headline.
Audio intent: a low, clean beat and a single restrained impact as the headline settles.
Audio-coupled idea: headline reveal near 3.70s strong cue; no rapid text typing.
Music: restrained upbeat business bed.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The vault — 4s
Recreate the app's actual mobile sandbox UI: STASH header, “14 DAYS” streak, “Liquidity Vault $12,450.00,” pink “Bali Getaway 84% Saved,” and the original Coffee & Boba ledger row. No real account data; these are source-code demo values.
Sequential/interaction: UI pieces arrive as one phone mockup, then balance and bucket settle.
Audio intent: calm confidence; slight card landing accent.
Audio-coupled idea: subtle motion on the vault; align a UI detail with a nearby beat.
Music: same low bed.
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — One tap — 4s
Show the real lime “Log $6.50 Boba” sandbox button. A simple cursor moves in and clicks. A small caption reads “Coffee. Boba. Logged.”
Sequential/interaction: simulate one click, with a single clear click sound.
Audio intent: dry click; music stays underneath.
Audio-coupled idea: click is the key action.
Music: same low bed.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Receipt, meet reality — 5s
Show the product result: Liquidity Vault updates to “$12,443.50”; “Matcha Latte” appears atop Recent Ledger with “-$6.50” and the existing “UNUSUAL spend” label. Hold long enough to read both. Supporting line: “Zero bank syncs.”
Sequential/interaction: balance updates, then ledger row slides into place.
Audio intent: one quiet reveal hit on the completed log.
Audio-coupled idea: result settles on the 12.65s strong cue; ledger is readable, not rapid-fire.
Music: same low bed.
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — The thesis — 4s
Finish with “NO BANK LOGINS. NO SPREADSHEETS.” and the STASH wordmark, on a clean warm-white grid with a lime underline and black hard shadow.
Sequential/interaction: two copy lines appear in order; hold both with the wordmark.
Audio intent: music resolves softly; no big logo boom.
Audio-coupled idea: final wordmark can receive a subtle accent near 19.48s if it does not compromise the hold.
Music: fade down at the end.
Transition mood: soft fade → end
